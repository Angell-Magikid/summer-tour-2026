Why This Tour?

What You'll Actually Experience

No textbooks. No lectures. Just real-world access to the companies and technologies that are reshaping our world. Every day is packed with hands-on experiences you can't get anywhere else in America.

🦾

Meet Humanoid Robots Face-to-Face

Watch Fourier Intelligence's GR-1 humanoid robot walk, talk, and perform tasks in real time. At Engine AI, see how next-gen actuators and AI models make robots move like humans. These aren't demos on YouTube — they're live, up close, and you can ask the engineers how they built them.

🚁

Inside DJI — The Drone Empire

Step inside DJI's global headquarters in Shenzhen — the company that owns over 70% of the world's consumer drone market. Get hands-on flight training, tour their R&D labs, learn about computer vision and autonomous navigation, and pilot the latest drones yourself.

🔩

Huaqiangbei: The Hardware Hacker's Paradise

Navigate the world's largest electronics market — 1,500+ stalls spanning 70+ buildings. Learn how hardware startups source components for $1 that cost $10 back home. Build your own mini robot prototype using parts you pick out yourself.

🌏

Live Like a Local — Culture Meets Tech

Ride the world's fastest elevator in Shanghai Tower (128 floors, 45 seconds). Browse books in a cloud-level bookstore. Soak in ancient hot springs in Zhuhai. Taste authentic dim sum, xiaolongbao, and Cantonese cuisine. Experience a culture where technology and tradition coexist beautifully.

🎓

Internship & Research Pathways

This isn't just sightseeing — it's career building. Meet hiring managers and research directors who are actively looking for bright American students to join their teams as interns, research assistants, and future engineers. Bring your resume.

🤝

Your Global Network Starts Here

You'll travel alongside a curated group of ambitious students who share your passion for AI and robotics. Make friends across cultures, connect with Chinese engineering mentors, and build relationships that could lead to university recommendations, research collaborations, or startup cofounders down the road.

  • Arrive at Shanghai Pudong Airport (PVG) — meet tour guides at arrivals hall
  • Hotel check-in near The Bund waterfront district
  • Afternoon orientation: team briefing, safety guidelines, tour welcome pack distribution
  • Evening stroll along The Bund — colonial architecture meets futuristic Pudong skyline

Tue
Day 2
📍 Shanghai — Zhangjiang Hi-Tech Park

🦿 Fourier Intelligence: Humanoid Robot R&D Deep Dive

  • Visit Zhangjiang Hi-Tech Park — China's premier innovation hub
  • Live demonstration of GR-1 General Purpose Humanoid Robot — walking, balancing, task execution
  • Lab tour: Rehabilitation robotics exoskeleton systems used globally
  • Hands-on workshop: Control a robotic arm via simple programming interface + Q&A session
Jul 9
Wed
Day 3
📍 Shanghai — Lujiazui & Pudong

🏙️ Shanghai's Icons: Engineering Marvels & Cultural Treasures

  • Shanghai Tower — ascend the world's 2nd tallest building (632m / 2073ft)
  • World's Fastest Elevator — 18 m/s (40 mph!) ride to floor 118 observation deck
  • Duoyun Bookstore (朵云书院) + China Art Museum (中华艺术宫) — culture and art immersion
  • AI Dream (AI之梦) — Large-Scale Immersive AI Interactive Experience: A groundbreaking theatrical production where you step inside a future world shaped by artificial intelligence. Walk through interactive scenes, engage with AI characters, and explore how technology will transform human life in the decades to come.
  • VR Interactive Experience Hall + Huangpu River evening cruise

Thu
Day 4
📍 Shanghai → Shenzhen (High-Speed Rail) → Songshan Lake XbotPark

🚄 Speed Into China's Silicon Valley — Shenzhen & XbotPark

  • Fuxing high-speed train — cruise at 350 km/h (217 mph), arrive in Shenzhen
  • XbotPark Robot Base (松山湖机器人基地) — Visit the legendary robotics incubator founded by Prof. Zexiang Li (李泽湘). See 60+ startup teams, shared smart factories, and the "5-minute ecosystem" where ideas become products
  • Innovation Academy tour: Hands-on demos of cutting-edge robots from cloud whale (云鲸) to smart agricultural machines. Meet young entrepreneurs and learn how hardware startups are built from scratch
  • Hotel check-in in Nanshan District — evening explore Talent Park & SEA WORLD, Shenzhen skyline

🔩 Huaqiangbei: The Ultimate Hardware Scavenger Hunt

  • Guided scavenger hunt through Huaqiangbei Electronics World — find sensors, motors, microcontrollers
  • Rapid prototyping workshop: Build a mini robot using scavenged parts + Arduino/ESP32 kits
  • Team competition: Whose mini-bot wins the obstacle course?
